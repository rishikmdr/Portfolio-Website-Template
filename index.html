About Me

Everyone is told to "use AI". Few businesses know where it actually pays off. I help founders and teams answer that question and then act on it.

I break a business into its core processes, find the repetitive, slow or error-prone work, and design AI workflows that fix it, with costs, risks and a step-by-step rollout plan. When you're ready, I build them: AI assistants, automations, data pipelines and dashboards.

For 17+ years I've led enterprise data and AI programs, from scoping and proposals to delivery, and built teams of up to 50 across data engineering, BI and AI. So my advice is practical, not hype.
