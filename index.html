Business Challenge
Each project moved through questionnaire design, scripting, fieldwork, tabulation and reporting by hand. End to end, that took three to four months or more.
My Role
Led the program as Lead Program Manager, from client discovery and solution design to delivery, with a cross-functional team across product, data engineering, data visualisation and AI engineering.

Outcome
The full workflow cut from an estimated three to four months to a few weeks. Researchers spend their time on judgement, not assembly.

Business Challenge
Bids arrived by email, phone and through agents, many for the same cargo. Tracking what was in the pipeline and in transit was hard.
My Role
Ran discovery with executive stakeholders and designed the prototype.
Solution
A bid console where each opportunity gets one record. An AI agent flags duplicate bids for the same cargo and suggests the best, cheapest or fastest route. A one-page executive view shows cargo in the pipeline and in transit by office, location and agent, and a role-based assistant answers each user's questions about their own data.

Leadership could see how AI fits their operation before investing in a full build.

Approach

How I Work

A simple, repeatable path from business problem to working solution.

  1. Discover

    Understand business goals, workflows, stakeholders and pain points.

  2. Prioritise

    Evaluate opportunities by value, feasibility, data readiness and risk.

  3. Design

    Define the solution, architecture, operating model and roadmap.

  4. Build and Validate

    Test against agreed success criteria, with users involved throughout.

  5. Scale and Improve

    Support adoption, monitor performance and improve over time.

Responsible by Design. Every solution keeps a person accountable for important decisions, protects personal and client data, is evaluated against agreed measures before rollout, and comes with clear ownership and governance.
